what's in the box

stages that don't repeat

every stage is generated fresh: forests, farmland, alpine passes, desert, tundra. there are real roads too, from California, Colorado, the Alps and Washington, with their real shape and elevation. your co-driver calls all of it.

14 surfaces, all of them bad

tarmac in every state of decay, loose and packed gravel, mud, snow. potholes, tar snakes, washboard, cattle grids, fords and spills. the loose stuff gets dug up as the field goes through and the stones bounce off your car.

a sim under the jank

raycast suspension, a proper tire model, real drivetrains and limited-slips. clutches fade, turbos heat-soak, radiators get holed and money shifts cost you the engine. damage sticks around.

wheels feel it

force feedback for MOZA, Fanatec, Logitech, Thrustmaster and Simagic. you feel tire grip, caster and every pothole, and the wheel goes light when the front washes out.

race your friends

online through Steam lobbies or by IP, split-screen on one PC, push-to-talk that sounds like a CB radio, and your own music on the car radio for everyone to hear.

the garage

70-something old cars, each with its own quirks. engine parts, an ECU app on the garage computer, launch control, aero that actually does something, and a FUN tab for making your car look ridiculous.

six bosses, six builds

street-tuned cars with names, attitudes and things to say before the start. you can drive their cars too.

meet them

get in

download the installer, run it, drive. the game updates itself after that, so you only install once.
