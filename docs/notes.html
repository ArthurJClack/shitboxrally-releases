version 0.7.1.0

the latest stable build. older notes live on the releases page.

new

  • strap on a headset and climb into the driver's seat (still early)
  • a proper race track showed up in the woods, and it comes with laps
  • don't cut the corners, they're watching
  • the garage computer finally boots
  • and there's an app on it for messing with your engine
  • a whole lot more to bolt under the hood
  • the parts need each other now, so plan the build
  • launch control, flat shift and a little bang bang bang
  • new screens for the dash
  • wings, lips and diffusers that actually do something
  • your opponent has a few words for you before the start
  • the highway got dressed up

cars

  • the air pushes back now, and tucking in behind someone pays off
  • exhausts and intakes bring a little power too

fixed

  • loose badges, plates and lamps are bolted back on
  • no more flickering trim
  • tires stay inside the bodywork
  • the download got a bit smaller
  • a big round of bug fixes, less memory and a smoother framerate
  • the garage computer and ECU screens behave better
  • vr feels steadier
  • hood cam actually sits on the hood now, on every car (no more staring at your own wipers in trucks and lowered cars)

still in the box

  • a way to race where the gap is all that matters
  • a wide open road for when you just want to send it
  • 70-something old shitboxes
  • bosses with their own cars, and you can drive them too
  • a FUN tab for making your car look ridiculous
  • online multiplayer and split-screen
  • weather that changes, water that moves, damage that sticks
  • your own music on the car radio
