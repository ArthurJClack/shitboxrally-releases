the garage

60 old cars and a few bad decisions

every car has its own engine, gearing, steering ratio and quirks. weights are curb weight.

the bosses

street tuned, raceable, and you can drive them too.

boss 01

Peylix

Egon · Volks GTI Mk5 2.0T (2008)

Stage 2 Mk5 GTI on 92, catless downpipe, H&Q Sport Cup kit, 11 % tint. Daily driven, debadged, and he knows every road you don't.

boss 02

Venom

Gio Metro 1.0 Turbo (1994)

Gio Metro with a turbo three off a Sprint, a Stage 1 file, catless downpipe and a straight pipe. 1,600 lb, coilovers, half cage, faded paint. Drives it like he stole it.

boss 03

wake_up

White Rabbit · Volks Rabbit GTI Mk1 1.8 (1983)

Clean white Mk1 Rabbit GTI with a built 16-valve head, close-ratio box, plated diff and coilovers on 15 in BBZ three-piece wheels. Under 1,800 lb and smooth as anything.

boss 04

canary_09

Black Dog · Pleiades Legacy Outback 2.0T swap (1998)

First-gen Legacy Outback with a WRX turbo motor swapped in, Stage 2, catless, a big brake kit, gravel suspension and a cage. Black on black, and it never lifts on gravel.

boss 05

yeller7

Yellers Yeller car · Pontifex GTO 5.7 (2004)

Yeller's Yellow Jacket '04 GTO: the 350 hp LS1 and a six-speed on staggered 18s, 245s up front and fat 275s out back, with six-piston CTS-V calipers up front. Loud, very yellow, and sideways everywhere.

boss 06

muttt

Bug-Eyed Devil · Pleiades WRX Wagon (2002)

Silver '02 WRX wagon with everything done: built motor, Stage 2 on race gas, coilovers, close-ratio box, big brakes, on 17 in RPF1s with a black cannon out back, an STI scoop and yellow fogs. Limo tint with a visor, vampire fangs and silver devil horns. Looks like a joke until it's gone.
